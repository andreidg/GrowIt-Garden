- The preview should appear immediately after upload, even before analysis finishes.
- The image should be contained inside the scanner card and should not overflow.
- Use object-fit: cover or object-fit: contain.
- The preview should work on mobile and desktop.
- If the image cannot render, show the uploaded filename instead.

- Keep the existing scanner card style.
- Replace or update the dashed upload box after upload so the user can see:
  - uploaded image preview
  - filename or “Change photo” option
  - analysis status
- Do not leave the scanner visually unchanged after upload.

3. Analysis loading state
- After upload, show:
  “Analyzing your garden photo...”
- This should appear inside the scanner card, near or below the image preview.

4. Restore analysis dropdown/panel
- After analysis completes, show a small expandable dropdown, panel, or result card below the uploaded image preview.
- The panel title should be:
  “Photo analysis”
- The panel should show:
  - Sunlight: [predicted value] — [High/Medium/Low] confidence
  - Soil: [predicted value] — [High/Medium/Low] confidence
  - optional note if available

  “Sunlight: Part sun — Medium confidence”
  “Soil: Raised Bed — High confidence”
  “Please confirm or adjust these values before continuing.”

- The photo analysis must update the existing Step 2 form fields:
  - sunlight level
  - soil/container type

  “Please confirm or adjust this value.”

- Full sun

- Full shade

Soil/container prediction must map to one of:
- Raised Bed
- In-Ground Clay
- In-Ground Loam
- Container/Pots

- Still show the uploaded photo preview.
- Show this friendly message:
  “Photo analysis is unavailable right now. You can still enter your garden details manually.”
- Do not clear the uploaded image.
- Do not clear any manually entered fields.

- sunlight level

- garden dimensions
- pests
