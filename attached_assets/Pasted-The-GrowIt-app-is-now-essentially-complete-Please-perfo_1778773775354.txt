6. Sunlight levels and info tooltips
Check that the app supports:
- Full sun
- Part sun
- Part shade
- Full shade
- Dappled shade

Each option should have a working info tooltip or explanatory text.

Use these definitions:
- Full sun: The area gets 6+ hours of direct sunlight per day. Best for most vegetables, herbs, and sun-loving flowers.
- Part sun: The area gets 4–6 hours of direct sunlight per day. Often works well for plants that like the sun but may need protection from intense afternoon heat.
- Part shade: The area gets 2–4 hours of direct sunlight per day, or filtered light for much of the day. Good for plants that prefer some sun but not full exposure.
- Full shade: The area gets less than 2 hours of direct sunlight per day. This does not mean total darkness; it still needs bright, indirect outdoor light.
- Dappled shade: Sunlight is filtered through trees or structures, creating shifting patches of light and shade. Common under trees or near fences/pergolas.
